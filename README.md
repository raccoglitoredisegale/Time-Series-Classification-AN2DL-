🏴‍☠️ AN2DL Challenge 1: The Pirate Pain DatasetCourse: Artificial Neural Networks and Deep Learning (Politecnico di Milano)Task: Multivariate Time Series ClassificationObjective: Predict pain levels based on motion data.🧭 Project OverviewIn this assignment, we are presented with a collection of multivariate time series data captured from ordinary people and pirates. Each sequence consists of 180 time steps containing temporal dynamics of body joints and pain perception.The objective is to perform Time Series Classification to assign each sequence to one of three possible classes representing the subject's true pain status:no_painlow_painhigh_pain📂 Dataset DescriptionEach record represents a time step within a subject’s recording, identified by sample_index and time.FilesFile NameDescriptionpirate_pain_train.csvThe training set data.pirate_pain_train_labels.csvLabels for the training set.pirate_pain_test.csvTest set (unlabeled).sample_submission.csvExample format for submission.Feature GroupsThe dataset contains several input channels:pain_survey_1 – pain_survey_4: Simple rule-based sensor aggregations estimating perceived pain.n_legs, n_hands, n_eyes: Subject physical characteristics.joint_00 – joint_30: Continuous measurements of body joint angles (neck, elbow, knee, etc.) across 180 time steps.⚠️ Constraints & RulesCrucial Rule: The use of pretrained models is FORBIDDEN.All neural networks must be trained from scratch. We are not allowed to rely on any pre-existing architectures or pretrained weights (e.g., ImageNet weights, pretrained Transformers). The architecture design and training pipeline must be developed entirely by the team.📊 EvaluationMetricsThe performance of the classification model is evaluated using the F1-Score.$$F1 = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{precision} \cdot \text{recall}}{\text{precision} + \text{recall}}$$Leaderboard: During the competition, submissions are evaluated on a subset of the test data.Final Score: Calculated on a separate, hidden test subset after the competition closes.Submission FormatPredictions must be submitted in a .csv file with the following format:Code snippetsample_index,label
